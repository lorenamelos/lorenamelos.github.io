About

(04)

Measure rather than assume. One variable at a time. Document what you decided not to do.

I came to data through metallurgical engineering, and before that, years as a visual artist. Both are disciplines of tolerance and irreversibility — you specify before you cut, and there is no undo. That is the through-line: the work I trust is the work that was measured, and the numbers I publish are the ones I can defend under a follow-up question.

Today I work across forecasting, NLP and LLM systems, and the pipelines that carry them. The part I find most interesting is not the model — it is what happens when the model is wrong and nothing raises an exception.

Currently open to remote roles and consulting through AIRA Labs, my applied AI studio. Based in Belo Horizonte, working with distributed teams.
